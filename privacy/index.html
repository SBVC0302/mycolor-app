隱私權政策

更新日期：2026-09-09 · English

營運與聯絡

MyColor 由 MyColor 開發團隊營運。隱私、帳號刪除、內容檢舉與支援請聯絡 mycolor.support@gmail.com。此政策說明目前免費、含廣告版本的資料處理方式。

帳號、好友與通知

登入會使用 Apple 或 Google 提供的識別碼、電子郵件，以及您設定的名稱、帳號、性別與頭像。好友關係、封鎖名單、群組成員、在線狀態、推播裝置 token 與通話信令用來提供聯絡功能。名稱、帳號、頭像與性別作為社交基本資料；電子郵件及裝置 token 不供其他使用者讀取。

照片、聊天、語音與日記

您分享的文字、照片、影片、語音、日記及回應會與帳號關聯儲存於 Firebase 與我們的後端。非隱藏日記供已接受的好友查看；群組內容供目前成員查看。公開頭像可被其他人下載。聊天室與日記媒體下載需驗證權限；隱藏、收回或移除權限後會拒絕後續下載，但不能收回他人已另存、截圖或轉傳的副本。服務採用傳輸加密及存取控制，未提供端對端加密。

AI 照片分析與處理者

開始 AI 分析前，App 會另外說明並取得同意。照片會縮圖、移除 EXIF，透過後端送往 OpenAI 進行品質檢查；色彩分析可能使用 APIYI 轉送的 Claude 模型，或 Anthropic 直接 API 備援。供應商依分析需要接收照片、提示內容及分析結果，臉部照片可能識別身分。分析結果與帳號關聯保存；本機取色與彩妝包不需要 AI 照片同意。您可在設定撤回後續 AI 傳送。已發送的請求無法收回。

AI 紀錄保留

我們的 AI 代理不主動永久保存分析原始照片；分析結果、請求狀態與防濫用紀錄會依功能保存。外部 AI 服務可能保存輸入、輸出、安全及法律要求的紀錄，期限依供應商、使用的模型與實際合約而異；不保證零保留、即時刪除或所有中介處理者採用相同政策。我們不把分析照片、聊天內容或色彩特徵傳給廣告商作為廣告素材或受眾資料。

即時通話與語音辨識

Agora 處理即時通話音訊、通話識別與必要的網路診斷資料。App 未啟用通話錄音服務；您另外發送的語音訊息則會保存。啟用語音轉文字時會使用 Apple Speech，音訊可能由 Apple 伺服器處理，視裝置與辨識語言支援而定。拒絕麥克風或語音辨識權限會影響對應功能。

廣告、使用分析與診斷

Google AdMob 可能處理裝置及廣告識別碼、IP 推估的大概位置、廣告曝光／互動、效能與診斷資訊，用於廣告、衡量及服務維護。需要追蹤許可時會使用 iOS ATT；可在系統設定變更。選擇性的使用分析預設關閉，可在設定變更；啟用後 Firebase Analytics 記錄功能互動與安裝識別，程式不附加帳號 ID、照片或色碼。Firebase Crashlytics 診斷可能與帳號識別碼的雜湊關聯。拒絕追蹤不代表完全不傳送廣告或安全診斷資料。

本機工具與匯出

拍照選色收藏、個人色卡的本機快取、彩妝包、開封／到期日、使用紀錄與提醒主要保存在此裝置。備份匯出檔可能包含產品資料、照片縮圖與文字；只有您操作匯出／分享後，檔案才交給選擇的目的地。請妥善保存並自行刪除匯出副本。 報告金鑰可另行匯出成密碼保護的備份；檔案與密碼由你保管，不會上傳給 MyColor。匯入時會驗證相同帳號與目前雲端報告，並在裝置上復原金鑰。若沒有原裝置、有效備份或密碼，舊報告可能無法解密。

安全、審查與服務供應商

Firebase／Google、後端託管與 PostgreSQL 服務商、AI 處理者及 Agora 依功能處理必要資料，可能涉及跨境傳輸。檢舉與內容審查由授權管理員查看處理所需內容；不保證所有不當內容都能自動辨識。私人媒體在 App 中不使用可長期離線讀取的快取，播放暫存受裝置檔案保護。任何系統都無法保證絕對安全。

保留、刪除與權利

帳號與您發送的內容通常保存至您刪除內容或帳號。刪除帳號需重新驗證，Apple 登入亦會撤銷授權；伺服器分階段清除 SQL、Firestore、Storage 與登入身分，遇到故障會重試，App 顯示實際進度。為提供後續查詢、防止舊請求重建帳號及故障稽核，最少量的帳號識別碼、刪除狀態、時間與查詢憑證雜湊仍會保留，不含已刪除的照片或聊天內容。供應商日誌、備份與依法保留的紀錄適用各自期限，不能保證同步清除。您可在設定撤回 AI／使用分析同意、匯出本機工具資料、刪除帳號，或聯絡支援提出存取、更正與刪除要求。

年齡與政策更新

本服務不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。若發現兒童資料或安全問題，請聯絡支援。資料處理方式變更時會更新政策；新增需要同意的資料用途時，會先提供說明與必要的選擇。

內容審查與申訴

未通過的內容可在 7 天內提出一次申訴；重新送審後的新決定可另行申訴。被拒絕內容的審查副本通常保留 7 天，若有待處理申訴，最長保留至拒絕後 30 天，再由背景工作清除。檢舉、處置與申訴紀錄用來處理安全問題，刪除帳號時依刪除流程清除。可在 App 的安全與內容審查頁查看處理狀態，審查回覆時間為營運目標而非保證。社交分享或 AI 服務可能在營運條件尚未確認時暫停開放；目前版本暫未開放通話。

Privacy policy

Operator and contact

MyColor is operated by the MyColor development team. Contact mycolor.support@gmail.com for privacy, deletion, safety reports and support. This policy covers the free app with advertising.

Account and social data

Apple or Google sign-in provides identifiers and email. We store your chosen name, handle, gender and avatar, friendships, blocks, group membership, presence, device push tokens and call signaling. Social profile fields are visible to other users; email and device tokens are private.

Shared content

Messages, photos, videos, voice messages, diaries and reactions are stored with your account in Firebase and our backend. Visible diaries are available to accepted friends and group content to current members. Avatars are public. Private media downloads require authorization; hiding, recalling or removing access stops future downloads. We cannot recall copies others have saved, captured or forwarded. Transport encryption and access controls are used; the service does not provide end-to-end encryption.

AI photo processing

AI analysis requires separate consent. Resized photos with EXIF removed are sent through our backend to OpenAI for quality checks. Color analysis may use APIYI to relay requests to Claude models or Anthropic directly as a fallback. Providers receive photos, prompts and outputs necessary for analysis. Faces may identify people and results are linked to accounts. Local color picking and makeup tools do not require AI photo consent. You can stop future AI transfers in Settings; sent requests cannot be recalled.

AI retention

Our proxy does not intentionally retain original analysis photos permanently. Results, request status and abuse-prevention records may be stored. Provider retention of inputs, outputs and safety/legal records depends on the provider, model and contract. We do not promise zero retention or immediate deletion at every intermediary. We do not send analysis photos, message contents or color traits to advertisers as advertising content or audience data.

Calls and speech recognition

Agora processes live call audio, call identifiers and network diagnostics. The app does not enable call recording, but voice messages you send are stored. Optional transcription uses Apple Speech and may process audio on Apple servers depending on device and language support. Microphone and speech permission choices affect these features.

Advertising and diagnostics

Google AdMob may process device/ad identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad exposure and interactions, performance and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and maintenance. iOS ATT is requested when required for tracking. Optional usage analytics is off by default and can be changed in Settings; Firebase Analytics receives installation identifiers and feature events without an attached account ID, photo or color code. Crashlytics diagnostics may be associated with a hashed account identifier. Declining tracking does not stop every advertising or security-related data transfer.

Local tools and exports

Saved swatches, local palette caches, makeup items, opening and expiry dates, usage and reminders are mainly stored on your device. Backup exports may include item details, thumbnails and notes. Exported data is sent to the destination you select; protect and delete those copies yourself. Report keys can be exported separately as password-protected backups. You keep the file and password; they are not uploaded to MyColor. Import validates the same account and current cloud report before restoring the device key. Without the original device, a valid backup or its password, the old report may be unrecoverable.

Security, review and processors

Firebase/Google, hosting and PostgreSQL providers, AI processors and Agora process necessary data, potentially across borders. Authorized reviewers can inspect content needed for reports and moderation. Filtering cannot identify every unsafe item. Private media is not kept in a persistent offline-readable app cache; playback temporary files use device file protection. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Retention, deletion and choices

Account data and your content are generally retained until you delete them or your account. Account deletion requires reauthentication; Apple authorization is revoked when applicable. The server retries staged SQL, Firestore, Storage and Auth cleanup and the app displays actual progress. Minimal account identifiers, deletion status, timestamps and receipt hashes remain for status checks, recreation prevention and operational audit, without deleted photos or message contents. Provider logs, backups and legally required records follow their own retention periods. You can withdraw AI/analytics consent, export local tools, delete your account or contact support for access, correction and deletion requests.

Age and updates

The service is not directed to children under 13. Contact support about child data or safety concerns. We update this policy when practices change and explain new purposes and obtain consent where required.

Content review and appeals

You may appeal a rejection once within 7 days. A new decision after resubmission may be appealed separately. Rejected review copies are normally retained for 7 days, or up to 30 days after rejection while an appeal is pending, then removed by background maintenance. Reports, decisions and appeal records support safety operations and are removed through account deletion. View progress in the in-app safety center. Response times are operational targets, not guarantees. Social sharing or AI may remain unavailable until operational requirements are confirmed. Calls are not available in this release.
